## **Position Filled - Thank you to all the Applicants.**

**About Oak Clinic:**

Oak Clinic is a rapidly growing Naturopathic, family clinic located in Dawson Creek, British Columbia. We are dedicated to providing our patients with a wide variety of Products and Services beyond the basic care offered by traditional medical systems. Our comprehensive services include Naturopathic Consultations, Acupuncture, Massage Therapy, Lab Testing, and a Dispensary of professional-grade supplements and personalized botanical prescriptions.

As Oak Clinic has experienced significant growth in the past year, we are in need of additional staff to meet the high standards of patient care we strive for.

**Position: Part-Time Receptionist**

**Schedule:** 2 days per week, 9:00 am - 5:00 pm (\*flexible days, but must be consistent)

**Responsibilities:**

-   Schedule appointments efficiently
-   Answer phone calls and respond to emails promptly
-   Manage billing for services and supplements
-   Maintain the reception area and ensure cleanliness and organization

**Wage:** $22-25 per hour (depending on skill set and experience)

**Skills & Abilities:**

-   Proficiency in online booking and billing systems
-   Excellent verbal and written communication skills
-   Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to work effectively with others
-   Ability to remain calm and composed in high-stress or emergency situations
-   Quick problem-solving abilities and the capacity to work independently

**Culture at Oak Clinic:**

We foster a casual and enjoyable workplace environment while maintaining a serious commitment to our roles and responsibilities within the clinic. At Oak Clinic, we value self-motivated individuals who are eager to join our growing team. We enjoy light-hearted banter, tea and creating a positive atmosphere for our patients.

**How to Apply:**

If you are a self-motivated individual interested in becoming a part of our team, please submit your resume and cover letter detailing your availability and preferred days to [hello@oakclinic.ca](mailto:hello@oakclinic.ca). We look forward to welcoming a new member to our team at Oak Clinic!

> \* Note: Please include your availability in your cover letter and specify which days you are available to work consistently.
